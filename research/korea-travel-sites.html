3. UI patterns worth copying for your own travel guide

Five-topic tile row

Visit Korea puts Transportation, Food and Accommodations in one row of icons right under the hero. That's a one-tap way into each of your five topics.

One long city page

Trip.com's Seoul page runs About, then Attractions, then Food, then Transport, then Where to Stay, then Best Time, all on one page. This is the closest match to "city + food + hotels + transport".

Filter panel + map toggle

Michelin has a left sidebar of filters (cuisine, price, diet), a sort row and a list/map switch. Good for a restaurant or hotel listing page.

Search with suggestion chips

Creatrip's hero is a search bar with chips under it (Myeongdong, Hongdae, KTX, AREX), so new visitors don't face an empty box.

"First time here?" grid

Visit Seoul and Visit Jeju both have a beginner block with visa, transport cards, maps and tax refund. It works well as a tile grid.

Rated cards with badges

Trazy and Trip.com cards show the rating and review count, a "From US$" price and badges like "Instant Booking". It's the standard look for listing cards.
